# Calculemus

*characteristica universalis*

- **When:** Leibniz, 1685 (after Llull’s *Ars*, c. 1305)
- **Who:** G. W. Leibniz
- **Telos:** Truth
- **Family:** Rationalist & experimental
- **Presupposes:** [Calculation error](../framings/calculation.md) (primary)
- **Descends from:** [Scholastic disputation](disputatio.md)
- **Gave rise to:** [Double crux](double_crux.md)

Leibniz imagined a universal characteristic in which reasoning would be calculation. ‘When there are disputes among persons, we can simply say: let us calculate, without further ado, to see who is right.’ Disagreement would become an arithmetic error, detectable on paper. The dream has never been realised and has never died. It lives on in decision theory, Bayesian epistemology and the double crux.

## Moves
- Formalise terms
- Compute
- Locate the error
