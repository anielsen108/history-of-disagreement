# Sense of the meeting

*Quaker business method*

- **When:** Religious Society of Friends, 1650s–
- **Who:** Society of Friends
- **Telos:** Decision
- **Family:** Communal discernment
- **Presupposes:** [Incomplete discernment](../framings/discernment.md) (primary)

- **Gave rise to:** [Activist consensus process](consensus.md)

No vote is taken. The clerk listens for and minutes the ‘sense of the meeting’, the unity the gathered body discerns under divine guidance. A Friend who disagrees may ‘stand aside’ or may block. Friends read disagreement as discernment that is not yet finished. Silence is one of the meeting’s working tools. Most secular consensus process descends from this practice.

## Procedure
1. Worshipful silence
2. Each speaks once, to the meeting not to each other
3. Clerk proposes a minute; stand aside or stand in the way

## What it assumes
[Incomplete discernment](../framings/discernment.md): Truth is one and is discerned together.

## In practice
- **Third party:** The clerk, who discerns and minutes the sense of the meeting.
- **Ends well when:** Unity, or a recorded stand-aside.
- **How it fails:** Fails when interests conflict or shared faith is absent.

**Strongest critique** (Jane Mansbridge, Beyond Adversary Democracy (1980)): Unitary, consensual democracy works where interests are common. Where they conflict, it hides the conflict and favours the articulate.
