# Toulmin model

*claim–data–warrant*

- **When:** Toulmin 1958
- **Who:** Stephen Toulmin
- **Telos:** Truth
- **Family:** Argumentation theory
- **Presupposes:** [Error & ignorance](../framings/error.md) (primary), [Disputed reputable opinion](../framings/endoxa.md)
- **Descends from:** [Aristotelian dialectic](aristotelian_dialectic.md)
- **Gave rise to:** [Pragma-dialectics](pragma.md)

Practical argument is modelled jurisprudentially, not geometrically. A claim rests on data through a warrant, which has backing and is limited by a qualifier and rebuttals. Many disagreements turn out to sit at the warrant, the inference licence, and not at the data or the claim. Warrants are ‘field-dependent’, so disputes across fields need a meta-argument about which warrants govern.

## Moves
- Separate claim from data
- Surface the warrant
- Locate the disagreement: data, warrant, backing or qualifier

## Sources
