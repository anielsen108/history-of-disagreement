# Moral reframing

- **When:** Feinberg & Willer 2015
- **Who:** Matthew Feinberg; Robb Willer
- **Telos:** Victory
- **Family:** Therapeutic & dialogic
- **Presupposes:** [Moral foundations](../framings/foundations.md) (primary)
- **Descends from:** [Deliberative rhetoric](rhetoric.md)


People argue in the moral foundations of their own side, and so fail to persuade the other. Experiments find that framing a position in the opponent’s moral terms, such as environmentalism framed as purity or a liberal policy framed as patriotism, measurably increases support. This is Aristotle’s audience-adaptation with a psychometric instrument.

## Moves
- Identify the audience’s operative foundations
- Re-encode the argument in them
