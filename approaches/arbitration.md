# Sovereign arbitration

*right reason by appointment*

- **When:** Hobbes, *Leviathan*, 1651
- **Who:** Thomas Hobbes
- **Telos:** Decision
- **Family:** Parliament, court & debate hall
- **Presupposes:** [War & sovereignty](../framings/sovereign.md) (primary)
- **Descends from:** [Staged religious disputation](staged.md)


Since no one’s reason is ‘right reason’ by nature, ‘the parties must by their own accord set up for right reason the reason of some arbitrator or judge’. Without this, dispute degenerates toward war. Hobbes gives the purest procedural answer. The parties leave the disagreement unresolved and hand its outcome to an authority they have agreed in advance to obey.

## Moves
- Pre-commit to an arbiter
- Accept the determination as if it were right reason

## Sources
- Hobbes, Thomas. *Leviathan* (1651). Edited by Richard Tuck. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1996. — ch. 5
