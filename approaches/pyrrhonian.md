# Pyrrhonian equipollence

*isostheneia → epochē → ataraxia*

- **When:** Pyrrho c. 300 BCE; Aenesidemus 1st c. BCE; Sextus c. 200 CE
- **Who:** Sextus Empiricus
- **Telos:** Release
- **Family:** Greek & Roman
- **Presupposes:** [Undecidability](../framings/equipollence.md) (primary), [Attachment to views](../framings/attachment.md)
- **Descends from:** [Arguing both sides](utramque.md), [Antilogic](antilogic.md)
- **Gave rise to:** [Free conversation](conference.md)

Scepticism is ‘an ability to set out oppositions among things that appear and are thought of in any way at all’. Equal force on both sides yields suspension of judgment, and tranquillity follows ‘like a shadow’. Disagreement (diaphōnia) is the first of Agrippa’s five modes. When philosophers and ordinary people dispute with no way to decide, the sceptic suspends judgment and picks no side.

## Moves
- Oppose appearance to appearance, thought to thought
- Invoke the modes (dispute, regress, relativity, hypothesis, circularity)
- Suspend; live by appearances

## Sources
