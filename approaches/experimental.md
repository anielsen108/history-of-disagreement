# Experimental matter of fact

*nullius in verba*

- **When:** Royal Society, 1660s
- **Who:** Robert Boyle; the Royal Society
- **Telos:** Truth
- **Family:** Early-modern civic & scientific
- **Presupposes:** [Error & ignorance](../framings/error.md) (primary), [War & sovereignty](../framings/sovereign.md)
- **Descends from:** [Scholastic disputation](disputatio.md)
- **Gave rise to:** [Community of inquiry](peirce.md), [Adversarial collaboration](adv_collab.md)

After civil war over doctrine, natural philosophers moved dispute off contested causes and onto witnessed matters of fact. These were produced by machines such as the air-pump, attested by gentlemen, and reported in a modest style. Shapin and Schaffer show this was as much a political technology as an epistemic one: a way of making disagreement safe by giving it a narrow, public, inspectable object. Hobbes saw it as a recipe for faction.

## Moves
- Narrow the dispute to an observable
- Multiply witnesses
- Separate fact from hypothesis; tolerate disagreement about the latter

## Sources
