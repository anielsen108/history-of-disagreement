# Principled negotiation

*Harvard Negotiation Project*

- **When:** Fisher & Ury, 1981
- **Who:** Roger Fisher; William Ury
- **Telos:** Settlement
- **Family:** Negotiation, mediation & peacemaking
- **Presupposes:** [Interests beneath positions](../framings/interests.md) (primary)
- **Descends from:** [Integration](follett.md), [Strategic bargaining](game_bargaining.md)
- **Gave rise to:** [Difficult conversations](difficult.md)

The method has four rules. Separate the people from the problem. Focus on interests, not positions. Invent options for mutual gain. Insist on objective criteria. Know your BATNA. The epistemic core is the claim that positions are lossy encodings of interests, and that interests are more often compatible than positions are.

## Moves
- Ask ‘why?’ behind each position
- Brainstorm before evaluating
- Agree on standards before applying them

## Sources
- Fisher, Roger, and William Ury. *Getting to Yes: Negotiating Agreement Without Giving In*. Boston: Houghton Mifflin, 1981.
