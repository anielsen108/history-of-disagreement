# Havruta

*חברותא*

- **When:** Talmudic ideal; modern yeshiva practice
- **Who:** Paired Talmud study
- **Telos:** Formation
- **Family:** Rabbinic
- **Presupposes:** [Collision with error](../framings/collision.md) (primary), [Plurality willed from above](../framings/divine_plurality.md)
- **Descends from:** [Dispute for the sake of Heaven](machloket.md)


Two learners argue their way through a text aloud. ‘As iron sharpens iron, so two scholars sharpen one another’ (Taanit 7a). ‘Either companionship or death’ (Taanit 23a). Holzer and Kent analyse it as three paired practices: listening and articulating, wondering and focusing, supporting and challenging. The partners argue with the text more than with each other.

## Procedure
1. Read aloud; propose a reading
2. Partner challenges from the text
3. Alternate support and challenge

## What it assumes
[Collision with error](../framings/collision.md): Truth unchallenged becomes dead dogma.

## In practice
- **Third party:** The text, and behind it the teacher.
- **Ends well when:** A reading both partners can defend.
- **How it fails:** Cleverness for its own sake (pilpul).

**Strongest critique** (Maharal of Prague (16th c.)): Hair-splitting dialectic trains ingenuity at the expense of understanding and distorts the plain sense of the law.
