# Difficult conversations

*the three conversations*

- **When:** Stone, Patton & Heen, 1999
- **Who:** Douglas Stone; Bruce Patton; Sheila Heen
- **Telos:** Understanding
- **Family:** Negotiation, mediation & peacemaking
- **Presupposes:** [Identity](../framings/identity.md) (primary), [Relationship-level message](../framings/relationship_level.md)
- **Descends from:** [Principled negotiation](principled.md)


Every hard conversation is three conversations at once. The ‘what happened’ conversation is about truth, intent and blame. The feelings conversation is about what each person feels. The identity conversation asks what this says about me: am I competent, good, lovable? Shift from certainty to curiosity, from blame to contribution, from inferred intent to reported impact.

## Moves
- Disentangle intent from impact
- Map joint contribution instead of blame
- Name the identity quake

## Sources
- Stone, Douglas, Bruce Patton, and Sheila Heen. *Difficult Conversations: How to Discuss What Matters Most*. New York: Viking, 1999.
