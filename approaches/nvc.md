# Nonviolent Communication

*OFNR*

- **When:** Rosenberg, 1960s; CNVC 1984; book 1999
- **Who:** Marshall Rosenberg
- **Telos:** Understanding
- **Family:** Therapeutic & dialogic
- **Presupposes:** [Unmet needs](../framings/needs.md) (primary)
- **Descends from:** [Rogerian exchange](rogerian.md)


Every utterance can be heard as an expression of observation, feeling, need and request. Judgments, demands and diagnoses are ‘tragic expressions of unmet needs’. Needs are universal and never actually in conflict. Strategies are what collide. Disagreement is thus re-described at the level where, by hypothesis, it disappears.

## Moves
- Observe without evaluating
- Name feelings
- Connect feelings to needs
- Make a doable request

## Sources
- Rosenberg, Marshall B. *Nonviolent Communication: A Language of Life*. 2nd ed. Encinitas, CA: PuddleDancer Press, 2003.
