# Nonviolent Communication

*OFNR*

- **When:** Rosenberg, 1960s; CNVC 1984; book 1999
- **Who:** Marshall Rosenberg
- **Telos:** Understanding
- **Family:** Therapeutic & dialogic
- **Presupposes:** [Unmet needs](../framings/needs.md) (primary)
- **Descends from:** [Rogerian exchange](rogerian.md)


Every utterance can be heard as an expression of observation, feeling, need and request. Judgments, demands and diagnoses are ‘tragic expressions of unmet needs’. Needs are universal and never actually in conflict. Strategies are what collide. NVC redescribes disagreement at a level where, by its own hypothesis, conflict cannot exist.

## Procedure
1. Observe without evaluating
2. Name feelings
3. Connect feelings to needs
4. Make a doable request

## What it assumes
[Unmet needs](../framings/needs.md): Beneath interests lie universal human needs: security, identity, recognition, autonomy, meaning.

## In practice
- **Third party:** Optional mediator trained in NVC.
- **Ends well when:** Needs named and a request accepted.
- **How it fails:** Scripted empathy; jargon used as a weapon.

**Strongest critique** (Critics in conflict studies): Recasting injustice as unmet need can depoliticise grievances and leave power untouched.


## Sources
