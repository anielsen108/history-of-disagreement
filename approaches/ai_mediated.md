# AI-mediated deliberation

*‘Habermas Machine’*

- **When:** Tessler et al. 2024
- **Who:** Google DeepMind team (Tessler et al.)
- **Telos:** Decision
- **Family:** Deliberative democracy & platforms
- **Presupposes:** [Affective polarisation](../framings/polarization.md) (primary), [Burdens of judgment](../framings/burdens.md)
- **Descends from:** [Bridging systems](polis.md), [Deliberative polling](delib_polling.md)


A language model drafts group statements from individual opinions, collects critiques, and revises. In UK experiments the AI-mediated statements were preferred over those of human mediators, and they reduced within-group division. This raises an old question in new form: whose ‘common ground’ does the mediator find, and what does it leave out?

## Moves
- Collect private opinions
- Model drafts candidate consensus
- Participants critique; model revises; vote

## Sources
- Tessler, Michael Henry, et al. “AI Can Help Humans Find Common Ground in Democratic Deliberation.” *Science* 386, no. 6719 (2024).
