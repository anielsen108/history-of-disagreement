A working tool

Diagnose a dispute

Think of a disagreement you are in. Answer the questions. The tool locates the dispute in the nine domains, the way Hermagoras located a case at its stasis, and suggests methods built for that kind of trouble.

Answer at least one question with “yes” or “somewhat”.

Mismatch finder

Two people can disagree about the disagreement. Pick what each party thinks it is really about, and see where they talk past each other and which methods can hold both frames.

This is a heuristic built from the site’s data, not a clinical instrument. It ranks methods by how many of their presupposed views fall in the domains you marked, weighting a method’s primary view double and adding weight for a match with your goal.
