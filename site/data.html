Open data & your notes

Data and notes

Everything on this site is drawn from a small set of data files. They are free to reuse under CC BY 4.0. The notes you write on entries stay in this browser; you can export them here.

Download

  • hod.json: everything (methods, views, links, people, glossary, excerpts, paths, gaps, sources, and the axes).
  • methods.csv: one row per method, with telos, family, views, confidence, third party, success and failure.
  • views.csv: one row per view, with domain and the methods that presuppose it.
  • links.csv: the method → view edges, flagged primary and contested.
  • people.csv: people, lifetimes, regions and lines of influence.
  • sources.csv: the bibliography.

List-valued CSV cells are separated by semicolons. Dates are plotting years (negative = BCE); the when field gives the honest range.
