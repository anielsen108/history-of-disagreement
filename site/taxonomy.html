Taxonomy

The same corpus is drawn three ways. The wheel shows which diagnosis each method presupposes. The lineage map shows who descends from whom. The matrix shows everything at once.

Hierarchical edge bundling (Holten 2006). Threads are coloured by the method’s telos. Hover a coloured arc to light a whole telos or domain. Click any name to open its entry; Esc closes it.

One lane per family of practice. An edge means the later method descends from the earlier one or defines itself against it, as the elenchus against antilogic or the experimental matter of fact against the disputation. Hover to light a method’s full ancestry and descent. Click the legend to hide teloi. Scroll sideways on small screens.

Solid = primary presupposition; pale = secondary.
