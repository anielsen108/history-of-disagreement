# Plurality willed from above

- **Domain:** Contemplative
- **When:** Eruvin 13b; Talmud
- **Who:** The Rabbis
- **Remedy implied:** Record minority views; decide without declaring falsehood.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Dispute for the sake of Heaven](../approaches/machloket.md), [Havruta](../approaches/havruta.md)

‘These and these are the words of the living God.’ Contradictory rulings can both belong to revelation. Halakhic decision is necessary for practice, but the rejected opinion stays Torah, is studied, and may be revived. On this view, revelation unfolds through disagreement.
