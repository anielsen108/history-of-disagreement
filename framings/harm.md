# Harm to relationship

- **Domain:** Relational
- **When:** Zehr 1990
- **Who:** Howard Zehr; restorative and indigenous traditions
- **Remedy implied:** Repair; circles; reintegration.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Palaver & consensus](../approaches/palaver.md), [Restorative justice & circles](../approaches/restorative.md), [Mediation](../approaches/mediation.md)

Conflict and crime are wounds in a web of relationships, not abstract violations. The question is not ‘who is right?’ or ‘what rule was broken?’ but ‘who was hurt, and what do they need?’

## Sources
- Zehr, Howard. *Changing Lenses: A New Focus for Crime and Justice*. Scottdale, PA: Herald Press, 1990.
- Braithwaite, John. *Crime, Shame and Reintegration*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1989.
