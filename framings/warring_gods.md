# Value pluralism

- **Domain:** Axiological
- **When:** Weber 1917/19; Berlin 1958
- **Who:** Max Weber; Isaiah Berlin
- **Remedy implied:** Choose and own the choice; build institutions for coexistence.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Citizens’ assemblies](../approaches/citizens.md)

The ultimate values of life are irreconcilable. ‘The various value spheres of the world stand in irreconcilable conflict with each other.’ Berlin adds that goods are many and cannot be measured on one scale. They conflict, so every choice entails a tragic loss. On this view, disagreement can be accurate: the parties correctly see that their values are incompatible.

> “Many old gods ascend from their graves… and resume their eternal struggle with one another.” — Weber, Science as a Vocation

## Sources
