# Contested validity claims

- **Domain:** Linguistic
- **When:** Habermas 1981
- **Who:** Jürgen Habermas
- **Remedy implied:** Discourse; ideal speech situation.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Coffeehouse & public sphere](../approaches/coffeehouse.md), [Discourse ethics](../approaches/discourse_ethics.md)

Speech acts implicitly claim truth, rightness and sincerity. Disagreement occurs when a hearer rejects one of these claims. The rejected claim shows which kind of argument is needed: theoretical discourse for truth, practical discourse for rightness, and consistent conduct over time for sincerity.

## Sources
- Habermas, Jürgen. *The Theory of Communicative Action* (1981). Translated by Thomas McCarthy. 2 vols. Boston: Beacon Press, 1984–87. — vol. 1, ch. 3
