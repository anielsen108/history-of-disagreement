# Threat & defensiveness

- **Domain:** Psyche
- **When:** Rogers 1951; Rapoport 1960; Miller & Rollnick
- **Who:** Carl Rogers; Anatol Rapoport
- **Remedy implied:** Restate before rebutting; reduce threat; evoke rather than argue.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Rogerian exchange](../approaches/rogerian.md), [Rapoport’s rules & region of validity](../approaches/rapoport.md), [Motivational interviewing](../approaches/mi.md), [Deep canvassing](../approaches/deep_canvass.md)

A person who feels threatened cannot hear. Evaluation is experienced as threat, and threat produces rigidity. Many disagreements persist because each side’s argument raises the other’s defences. The obstacle is affective, not logical.

## Sources
- Rogers, Carl R. “Communication: Its Blocking and Its Facilitation.” *ETC: A Review of General Semantics* 9 (1952): 83–88. (Delivered at Northwestern University, 1951.)
- Rapoport, Anatol. *Fights, Games, and Debates*. Ann Arbor: University of Michigan Press, 1960.
