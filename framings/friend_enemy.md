# Friend & enemy

- **Domain:** Power
- **When:** Schmitt 1932
- **Who:** Carl Schmitt
- **Remedy implied:** Decision, not debate (Schmitt); or agonistic taming (Mouffe).
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Agonistic pluralism](../approaches/agonism.md)

The specifically political distinction is between friend and enemy. Any opposition, whether religious, moral or economic, becomes political when it is intense enough to group people as friends and enemies. Liberal discussion hopes to dissolve the political into endless conversation and therefore misunderstands it.

## Sources
- Schmitt, Carl. *The Concept of the Political* (1932). Translated by George Schwab. New Brunswick, NJ: Rutgers University Press, 1976.
