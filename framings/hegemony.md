# Hegemony

- **Domain:** Power
- **When:** Gramsci; Laclau & Mouffe; Mouffe 2000
- **Who:** Chantal Mouffe
- **Remedy implied:** Agonistic institutions; counter-hegemonic struggle.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Agonistic pluralism](../approaches/agonism.md)

Every social order is a contingent hegemonic articulation, and every consensus excludes. Disagreement marks what the current settlement suppresses. Mouffe’s aim is to give it legitimate expression.

## Sources
- Mouffe, Chantal. *The Democratic Paradox*. London: Verso, 2000.
- Mouffe, Chantal. *On the Political*. London: Routledge, 2005.
