# Mimetic rivalry

- **Domain:** Psyche
- **When:** Girard 1961–72
- **Who:** René Girard
- **Remedy implied:** Recognise the double; refuse the scapegoat.
- **Methods presupposing it:** none in this survey

We desire what the model desires, so imitation makes rivals of those who are alike. As rivalry escalates, the object disappears and the rivals become doubles, each mirroring the other’s violence. Communities resolve the crisis by uniting against a scapegoat.

## Sources
- Girard, René. *Violence and the Sacred* (1972). Translated by Patrick Gregory. Baltimore: Johns Hopkins University Press, 1977.
