# Constructive difference

- **Domain:** Generative
- **When:** Follett 1925
- **Who:** Mary Parker Follett
- **Remedy implied:** Integration over compromise.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Activist consensus process](../approaches/consensus.md), [Integration](../approaches/follett.md)

Follett asks us to think of conflict as friction in an engine. In itself it is neither good nor bad. It is the appearance of difference, and invention starts from difference. Integration produces something new that neither party brought.

## Sources
- Follett, Mary Parker. “Constructive Conflict” (1925). In *Dynamic Administration: The Collected Papers of Mary Parker Follett*, edited by Henry C. Metcalf and L. Urwick. New York: Harper, 1940.
