# Developmental form

- **Domain:** Psyche
- **When:** Kegan 1994; Chapman
- **Who:** Robert Kegan; David Chapman
- **Remedy implied:** Hold your system as object; ask what each frame is for.
- **Methods presupposing it:** none in this survey

The same disagreement takes a different shape depending on how a person makes meaning. At a ‘self-authoring’ order, one is one’s ideology and conflict is between systems. At a ‘self-transforming’ order, one can hold one’s own system as object, so conflict becomes a sign of over-identification with a partial form. Chapman’s ‘meta-rationality’ treats the fit between frame and situation as the real question.
