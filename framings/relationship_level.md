# Relationship-level message

- **Domain:** Relational
- **When:** Watzlawick et al. 1967
- **Who:** Paul Watzlawick; Janet Beavin Bavelas; Don Jackson
- **Remedy implied:** Metacommunicate; change the pattern, not the topic.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Restorative justice & circles](../approaches/restorative.md), [Mediation](../approaches/mediation.md), [Difficult conversations](../approaches/difficult.md)

Every communication has a content aspect and a relationship aspect, and the latter classifies the former. Couples argue about the dishes while negotiating dominance and care. Each party also ‘punctuates’ the sequence differently (‘I withdraw because you nag’; ‘I nag because you withdraw’), so each sees the other as the cause.

## Sources
- Watzlawick, Paul, Janet Beavin Bavelas, and Don D. Jackson. *Pragmatics of Human Communication*. New York: Norton, 1967.
