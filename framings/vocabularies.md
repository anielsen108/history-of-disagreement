# Clash of final vocabularies

- **Domain:** Linguistic
- **When:** Rorty 1979, 1989
- **Who:** Richard Rorty
- **Remedy implied:** Redescription; irony; keep the conversation going.
- **Methods presupposing it:** none in this survey

Everyone carries a ‘final vocabulary’, words beyond which they have only circular argument. Evidence cannot settle a dispute between vocabularies. One side redescribes the other’s vocabulary in its own terms, or the conversation simply continues. Rorty’s edifying philosophy aims to keep the conversation going and makes no attempt to end it with truth.
