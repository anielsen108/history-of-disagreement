# Clash of final vocabularies

- **Domain:** Linguistic
- **When:** Rorty 1979, 1989
- **Who:** Richard Rorty
- **Remedy implied:** Redescription; irony; keep the conversation going.
- **Methods presupposing it:** none in this survey

Everyone carries a ‘final vocabulary’, words beyond which they have only circular argument. Disputes between vocabularies are not settled by evidence. One vocabulary is redescribed in terms of another, or the conversation simply continues. Edifying philosophy aims to keep the conversation going, not to end it with truth.
