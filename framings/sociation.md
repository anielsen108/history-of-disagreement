# Conflict as sociation

- **Domain:** Generative
- **When:** Simmel 1908; Coser 1956
- **Who:** Georg Simmel; Lewis Coser
- **Remedy implied:** Institutionalise, don’t suppress; foster cross-cutting cleavages.
- **Methods presupposing it:** none in this survey

Conflict is a form of social relation, not its absence. It binds the antagonists, clarifies boundaries, and creates group cohesion. Cross-cutting conflicts can stitch a society together. A society without conflict would be a society without contact.

## Sources
- Simmel, Georg. *Conflict and the Web of Group-Affiliations*. Translated by Kurt H. Wolff and Reinhard Bendix. Glencoe, IL: Free Press, 1955. (From *Soziologie*, 1908, ch. 4, “Der Streit.”)
- Coser, Lewis A. *The Functions of Social Conflict*. Glencoe, IL: Free Press, 1956.
