# Argumentative reasoning

- **Domain:** Psyche
- **When:** Mercier & Sperber 2011; Kahan 2012
- **Who:** Hugo Mercier; Dan Sperber; Dan Kahan
- **Remedy implied:** Argue in groups with real diversity; decouple facts from identity.
- **Methods presupposing it:** none in this survey

Reasoning evolved to produce and evaluate arguments in social exchange, not to find truth alone. Individuals show myside bias when producing arguments and are better at judging others’ arguments. Groups that argue can outperform individuals. Kahan adds identity-protective cognition: numerate partisans polarise more on politicised facts, not less.

## Sources
