# Vanity & obstinacy

- **Domain:** Psyche
- **When:** the Aṭṭhakavagga; Montaigne; Schopenhauer
- **Who:** Schopenhauer; Montaigne; the Buddha of Sn 4
- **Remedy implied:** Lower the stakes of being wrong; or exploit them (eristic).
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Eristic](../approaches/eristic.md), [Refusing dispute](../approaches/atthaka.md), [Free conversation](../approaches/conference.md), [Eristic dialectic](../approaches/schopenhauer.md), [Delphi method](../approaches/delphi.md)

Most people cannot bear to be shown wrong. Once a position is stated, defending it becomes a matter of self-regard, and the original question is abandoned. Montaigne thought obstinacy and heat of opinion the surest sign of a weak mind.
