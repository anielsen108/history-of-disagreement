# Metalinguistic negotiation

- **Domain:** Linguistic
- **When:** Plunkett & Sundell 2013
- **Who:** David Plunkett; Tim Sundell
- **Remedy implied:** Make the normative question about usage explicit (conceptual engineering).
- **Methods presupposing it:** none in this survey

Many apparently factual disputes, such as whether Secretariat is an ‘athlete’ or whether waterboarding is ‘torture’, are disputes about how a word ought to be used. These disputes are real and important because words have practical consequences. So a dispute about words can matter as much as a dispute about facts.

## Sources
- Plunkett, David, and Tim Sundell. “Disagreement and the Semantics of Normative and Evaluative Terms.” *Philosophers’ Imprint* 13, no. 23 (2013): 1–37.
