# Struggle for recognition

- **Domain:** Relational
- **When:** Hegel 1807; Honneth 1992
- **Who:** G. W. F. Hegel; Axel Honneth
- **Remedy implied:** Recognition as remedy; attend to disrespect beneath the stated grievance.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Speculative dialectic](../approaches/hegel.md)

Self-consciousness exists only in being acknowledged, so the first conflict is a life-and-death struggle for recognition. Honneth turns this into a moral grammar of social conflict. Disrespect in love, rights and esteem drives struggles that present themselves as being about other things.

## Sources
- Hegel, G. W. F. *Phenomenology of Spirit* (1807). Translated by Terry Pinkard. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2018. — §§178–196
- Honneth, Axel. *The Struggle for Recognition* (1992). Translated by Joel Anderson. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 1995.
