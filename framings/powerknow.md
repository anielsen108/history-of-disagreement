# Power / knowledge

- **Domain:** Power
- **When:** Nietzsche 1887; Foucault 1976–80
- **Who:** Friedrich Nietzsche; Michel Foucault
- **Remedy implied:** Genealogy; problematisation; attention to who is authorised to speak.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Staged religious disputation](../approaches/staged.md)

Each society has its ‘regime of truth’, which governs the types of discourse it accepts as true and who is authorised to say what counts. Disputes over truth are also struggles over the rules that produce truth. Who may speak is decided before what is said.

## Sources
- Foucault, Michel. “Truth and Power.” In *Power/Knowledge: Selected Interviews and Other Writings, 1972–1977*, edited by Colin Gordon. New York: Pantheon, 1980.
- Nietzsche, Friedrich. *On the Genealogy of Morality* (1887). Translated by Carol Diethe. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2007.
