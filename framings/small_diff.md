# Narcissism of small differences

- **Domain:** Psyche
- **When:** Freud 1917–30
- **Who:** Sigmund Freud
- **Remedy implied:** Notice how close the opponent is.
- **Methods presupposing it:** none in this survey

Communities with adjoining territories, and those related to each other in other ways, are especially prone to feud and ridicule. Hostility is greatest between those who are nearly alike. Disagreement here does not track distance of views. It serves the group’s cohesion and its narcissism.

## Sources
- Freud, Sigmund. *Civilization and Its Discontents* (1930). Translated by James Strachey. New York: Norton, 1961. — ch. 5
