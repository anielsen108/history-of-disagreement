# Disorder of names

- **Domain:** Linguistic
- **When:** Confucius; Xunzi; Later Mohists
- **Who:** Confucius; Xunzi; the Mohists
- **Remedy implied:** Rectify names, by sage convention or by disputation.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Mohist disputation](../approaches/bian.md), [Rectification of names](../approaches/zhengming.md)

Disputes arise when names no longer track realities and roles. For the Mohists, every bian is about whether a name fits. For Xunzi, deviant sophists who ‘confuse names’ create disorder that a true king must correct.

> “If names are not correct, speech will not accord with things.” — Analects 13.3
