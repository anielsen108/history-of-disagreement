# Strife as engine

- **Domain:** Generative
- **When:** Heraclitus c. 500 BCE; Hegel 1807; Marx
- **Who:** Heraclitus; Hegel; Marx
- **Remedy implied:** Sharpen, then sublate, the contradiction.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Speculative dialectic](../approaches/hegel.md)

‘War is father of all and king of all.’ ‘Justice is strife.’ Opposition is the motor of becoming. Hegel makes contradiction the self-movement of the concept, and Marx locates it in material relations. Disagreement is how history and thought advance.
