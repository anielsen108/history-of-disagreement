# Unfinished inquiry

- **Domain:** Epistemic
- **When:** Peirce; Dewey
- **Who:** C. S. Peirce
- **Remedy implied:** Better experiments; patience; fallibilism.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Community of inquiry](../approaches/peirce.md)

Disagreement is the state of a community whose inquiry has not yet converged. Under the method of science, the opinion ‘fated to be ultimately agreed to by all who investigate’ is what we mean by truth. Disagreement is therefore temporal, not permanent.
