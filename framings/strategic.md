# Mixed-motive game

- **Domain:** Interest
- **When:** Nash 1950; Schelling 1960
- **Who:** Thomas Schelling; John Nash
- **Remedy implied:** Bargaining analysis; credible commitments; focal points.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Strategic bargaining](../approaches/game_bargaining.md), [Rapoport’s rules & region of validity](../approaches/rapoport.md)

Conflict is rarely pure. The parties have a common interest in avoiding mutual loss and a conflicting interest over how to divide gains. What looks like argument is often signalling, commitment and the search for a focal point.

## Sources
- Schelling, Thomas C. *The Strategy of Conflict*. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1960.
- Nash, John F. “The Bargaining Problem.” *Econometrica* 18, no. 2 (1950): 155–162.
