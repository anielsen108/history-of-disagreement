# ‘This’ and ‘that’

- **Domain:** Contemplative
- **When:** Zhuangzi, ch. 2
- **Who:** Zhuangzi
- **Remedy implied:** Walking two roads; illumination.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Walking two roads](../approaches/liangxing.md)

Every ‘this is so’ (shi) is made from a standpoint, and from the other side it is ‘that’ (bi). The Confucians and Mohists affirm what the other denies. The sage sees that ‘this’ and ‘that’ generate each other, and rests at the pivot from which both can be allowed.
