# Incommensurable paradigms

- **Domain:** Epistemic
- **When:** Kuhn 1962
- **Who:** Thomas Kuhn
- **Remedy implied:** Translation, exemplars and time. No crucial experiment will settle it.
- **Methods presupposing it:** none in this survey

Across a revolution, scientists ‘practice their trades in different worlds’. They disagree about which problems matter, what counts as a solution and what terms mean, so that there is no neutral algorithm of choice. Persuasion across paradigms resembles conversion.

## Sources
- Kuhn, Thomas S. *The Structure of Scientific Revolutions*. Chicago: University of Chicago Press, 1962. — chs. 10, 12
