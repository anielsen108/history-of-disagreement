# Unmet needs

- **Domain:** Psyche
- **When:** Burton 1990; Rosenberg 1999
- **Who:** John Burton; Marshall Rosenberg
- **Remedy implied:** Needs-based dialogue; ‘provention’ (Burton’s coinage).
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Interactive problem-solving workshop](../approaches/problem_solving.md), [Nonviolent Communication](../approaches/nvc.md)

Beneath interests lie universal human needs: security, identity, recognition, autonomy, meaning. These cannot be bargained over, but they can be satisfied, and they rarely truly conflict. Conflict is what unmet needs sound like.

## Sources
- Burton, John W., ed. *Conflict: Human Needs Theory*. New York: St. Martin’s Press, 1990.
- Rosenberg, Marshall B. *Nonviolent Communication: A Language of Life*. 2nd ed. Encinitas, CA: PuddleDancer Press, 2003.
