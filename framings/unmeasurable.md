# What cannot be measured

- **Domain:** Axiological
- **When:** Plato, Euthyphro
- **Who:** Socrates (Euthyphro 7b–d)
- **Remedy implied:** Find a measure, or accept enduring enmity.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Stasis theory](../approaches/stasis.md), [Court policy debate](../approaches/saltiron.md)

Socrates observes that people end disputes about number, size and weight by counting, measuring and weighing. The disputes that cause anger and enmity concern the just and unjust, the noble and shameful, the good and bad, for which we have no agreed standard of decision. It is the earliest explicit statement that value disagreements differ in kind.
