# Incomplete discernment

- **Domain:** Contemplative
- **When:** Quakers, 1650s–
- **Who:** Society of Friends
- **Remedy implied:** Wait in silence; seek unity, not majority.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Sense of the meeting](../approaches/quaker.md), [Activist consensus process](../approaches/consensus.md)

Truth is one and is discerned together. Persisting disagreement means the gathered meeting has not yet waited long enough for the Light. A lone objector may be right, which is why a block is honoured.

## Sources
- Sheeran, Michael J. *Beyond Majority Rule: Voteless Decisions in the Religious Society of Friends*. Philadelphia: Philadelphia Yearly Meeting, 1983.
