# Class interest & ideology

- **Domain:** Interest
- **When:** Marx & Engels 1848
- **Who:** Karl Marx; Friedrich Engels
- **Remedy implied:** Ideology critique; organising; changing the material base.
- **Methods presupposing it:** none in this survey

‘The history of all hitherto existing society is the history of class struggles.’ Ideas follow material positions, and the ruling ideas are the ideas of the ruling class. Many intellectual disagreements are class antagonisms in disguise.

## Sources
- Marx, Karl, and Friedrich Engels. *Manifesto of the Communist Party*. London, 1848.
