# Moral foundations

- **Domain:** Axiological
- **When:** Graham, Haidt & Nosek 2009; Haidt 2012
- **Who:** Jonathan Haidt and collaborators
- **Remedy implied:** Moral reframing; humility about one’s own foundations.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Moral reframing](../approaches/moral_reframing.md)

Moral judgment rests on a few intuitive foundations: care, fairness, loyalty, authority, sanctity and, later, liberty. Political camps weight these differently. Reasoning mostly follows intuition, like a rider on an elephant, so disputes conducted in one camp’s foundations do not land in the other’s.

## Sources
- Graham, Jesse, Jonathan Haidt, and Brian A. Nosek. “Liberals and Conservatives Rely on Different Sets of Moral Foundations.” *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology* 96, no. 5 (2009): 1029–1046.
- Haidt, Jonathan. *The Righteous Mind: Why Good People Are Divided by Politics and Religion*. New York: Pantheon, 2012.
