# War & sovereignty

- **Domain:** Power
- **When:** Hobbes 1651
- **Who:** Thomas Hobbes
- **Remedy implied:** Arbitration; delegation of judgment.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Stasis theory](../approaches/stasis.md), [Rectification of names](../approaches/zhengming.md), [Staged religious disputation](../approaches/staged.md), [Experimental matter of fact](../approaches/experimental.md), [Sovereign arbitration](../approaches/arbitration.md)

Private judgment about right and wrong is the seed of civil war. Dispute is a pre-political condition that only a sovereign arbiter can end. ‘Right reason’ is whatever reason the parties have agreed to treat as authoritative.

## Sources
- Hobbes, Thomas. *Leviathan* (1651). Edited by Richard Tuck. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1996. — chs. 5, 13, 18
