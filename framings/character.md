# Character & motive

- **Domain:** Relational
- **When:** Plato; Avot 5:17; Nyāya
- **Who:** Socrates; the Mishnah; Nyāya debaters
- **Remedy implied:** Purify motive; judge disputes by their spirit.
- **Methods presupposing it:** [Socratic elenchus](../approaches/elenchus.md), [Vāda (good-faith debate)](../approaches/vada.md), [Dispute for the sake of Heaven](../approaches/machloket.md)

Disagreement tests who you are. The Mishnah judges a dispute by whether it is ‘for the sake of Heaven’, and Socratic examination is examination of a life. The worth of the exchange lies in the disputants’ intention and virtue more than in its outcome.
